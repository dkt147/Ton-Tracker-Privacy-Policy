# Ton Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Ton Tracker is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information We Collect

### Account and profile information
- Email address, full name, and account credentials used for authentication.
- Profile details entered by the user, such as display name and optional profile image.

### Ticket and earnings data
- Ticket numbers, mill and driver details, dates, tonnage, and earnings records.
- Job history, driver assignments, and related delivery or transport details.

### Device and app usage data
- Local app settings and preferences, including theme and biometric login state.
- Local storage may retain session information and offline queued ticket entries required for app functionality.

### Permissions
The app may request access to the following device features for core functionality:
- Camera: to scan ticket barcodes/QR codes and verify ticket details.
- Photo library: to select a profile image, if that feature is enabled.
- Biometric authentication: to support fingerprint or face unlock for secure access.
- Network access: to sync ticket and earnings data with Firebase and other backend services.

## How Information Is Used

- To authenticate users and route them to the appropriate driver or admin experience.
- To store, display, search, and filter ticket records, job history, and earnings summaries.
- To support offline mode and sync queued ticket data when a connection is available.
- To provide biometric login and maintain user session state.
- To process optional payment or subscription functionality if enabled in the future.

## Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Ton Tracker uses Firebase services and may rely on the following providers:
- Firebase Authentication for user sign-in and identity management.
- Cloud Firestore for storing ticket, driver, and earnings data.
- Firebase Storage for any user-uploaded profile images or related files.
- Firebase Cloud Functions for backend tasks and data processing.
- Stripe for payment and subscription processing where applicable.
- Google ML Kit for text recognition and ticket scanning assistance.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

Ton Tracker does not sell or rent your personal information.

Information is only shared to the extent necessary to operate the app, including:
- Authentication providers and backend services required for app functionality.
- Payment processors for subscription or billing features.
- Law enforcement or legal authorities if required by law.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

Biometric authentication is handled by the device platform and no biometric data is stored by Ton Tracker.

## Your Choices

- You may choose not to provide optional profile data.
- You may disable biometric login and use email/password authentication instead.
- You may contact the app administrator to request data updates or account deletion.

## Children’s Privacy

Ton Tracker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. The latest version is always available in this file.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the app owner or development team.
